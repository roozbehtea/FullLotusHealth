What microneedling does

Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses fine, sterile, medical-grade needles to create tiny controlled channels in the skin. This prompts your skin's natural repair process, which builds new collagen over the following weeks.

What it can help with

  • Fine lines and wrinkles, including crow's feet
  • Acne scarring
  • Uneven texture and tone
  • Enlarged pores
  • Thinning hair along the hairline (scalp microneedling)

A session, start to finish

Sessions are 90 minutes, and your first visit includes an intake and a treatment plan. A numbing cream goes on first, and most people find the treatment comfortable. Some redness afterward, similar to a mild sunburn, usually settles within a day or two.

Results build over time. Most people do a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart, and your plan is set at your first visit.

Common questions

Does microneedling hurt?

A numbing cream is applied first, and most people find the treatment comfortable.

How long does redness last?

Expect some redness afterward, similar to a mild sunburn. It usually settles within a day or two.

How many sessions will I need?

Results build over time, and most people do a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart. Your first visit includes a treatment plan.

Do you offer microneedling for thinning hair?

Yes. Scalp microneedling along the hairline is offered. Individual results vary.

Microneedling or nanoneedling?

Microneedling works deeper, for scarring, lines, and thinning hair. Nanoneedling is a gentler surface treatment for glow and hydration, with no downtime.
