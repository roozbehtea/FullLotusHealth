Why microneedling

Natural anti-aging, from deep within the skin

Instead of adding something to your face, microneedling asks your skin to renew itself.

  • Deep rejuvenation

    Works below the surface to renew the whole face, not just polish the top layer.

  • Collagen and elasticity

    Triggers your skin's own repair process, so it makes fresh collagen and elastin. Skin tends to look firmer, smoother and more youthful as results build.

  • Nourished with herbs

    Herbal serums that deeply nourish the skin and support collagen growth naturally, chosen for your skin. Red light therapy is part of every session.

How it works

Wake up your skin's own renewal

  1. Fine channels, rich nourishment

    Fine, sterile, medical-grade needles create tiny controlled channels in the skin, and custom herbal serums are carried in.

  2. Your skin repairs

    This switches on your skin's natural repair process.

  3. Collagen builds

    Over the following weeks your skin builds new collagen and elastin, so it looks firmer, smoother and more youthful. Most people do a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart.

Common questions

Does microneedling hurt?

A numbing cream is applied first, and most people find the treatment comfortable.

How long does redness last?

Expect some redness afterward, similar to a mild sunburn. It usually settles within a day or two.

How many sessions will I need?

Results build over time, and most people do a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart. Your first visit includes a treatment plan.

Do you offer microneedling for thinning hair?

Yes. Scalp microneedling along the hairline is offered.
