What nanoneedling is

Nanoneedling is a gentle alternative to microneedling. It uses ultra-fine nano-tips that open microscopic channels in the very surface of the skin, without going deeper. Those channels let hyaluronic acid, vitamins, and custom Chinese herbal serums absorb where your skin needs them.

Who it suits

  • Dull, dehydrated, or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and uneven texture
  • Sensitive skin that doesn't do well with stronger treatments
  • A glow before an event
  • Maintenance between microneedling sessions

What happens at Full Lotus Health

Roozbeh Ghobadpour, L.Ac., a licensed acupuncturist with over 10 years of clinical experience, chooses the herbal serums for your skin, and red light therapy is included. Sessions are 60 minutes.

There's no downtime, so you can go straight back to your day. Skin often looks brighter right away and keeps improving over the following days. It works well on its own or as a regular series.

Nanoneedling or microneedling?

Nanoneedling is a surface-level treatment for glow and hydration. Microneedling works deeper, for scarring, lines, and thinning hair. If you're not sure which fits, book a session and we'll decide together, or text a question.

Can I do nanoneedling as a series?

Yes. It works well on its own or as a regular series.
