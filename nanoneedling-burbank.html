What nanoneedling is

Nanoneedling is a gentle alternative to microneedling. It uses ultra-fine nano-tips that open microscopic channels in the very surface of the skin, without going deeper. Those channels let hyaluronic acid, vitamins, and custom Chinese herbal serums absorb where your skin needs them.

Who it suits

  • Dull, dehydrated, or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and uneven texture
  • Sensitive skin that doesn't do well with stronger treatments
  • A glow before an event
  • Maintenance between microneedling sessions

What happens at Full Lotus Health

Roozbeh Ghobadpour, L.Ac., a licensed acupuncturist with over 10 years of clinical experience, chooses the herbal serums for your skin, and red light therapy is included. Sessions are 60 minutes.

There's no downtime, so you can go straight back to your day. Skin often looks brighter right away and keeps improving over the following days. It works well on its own or as a regular series.

Nanoneedling is also offered as the Five Element Rejuvenation™ series: five sessions, one for each element of Chinese medicine, each with its own signature serum.

Common questions

Is there downtime after nanoneedling?

No. You can go straight back to your day.

How long is a session?

Sixty minutes.

Nanoneedling or microneedling?

Nanoneedling is a surface-level treatment for glow and hydration. Microneedling works deeper, for scarring, lines, and thinning hair. If you're not sure which fits, book a session and we'll decide together, or text a question.

Can I do nanoneedling as a series?

Yes. It works well on its own or as a regular series, and the five-session Five Element Rejuvenation™ series is the simplest way to do it.

Signature series

Five Element Rejuvenation™

The same five-element approach as a gentler series, with no downtime. Five sessions, one for each element of Chinese medicine, each with its own focus and its own signature serum, mixed into a hyaluronic acid base in the clinic and carried into the skin's surface.

The series follows the generating cycle, from Wood to Water, so each session builds on the one before. Nanoneedling sessions can be closer together than microneedling, and your plan sets the pace.

  1. Session 1

    Wood

    Release

    Tension and expression lines

    In Chinese medicine, Wood governs smooth, easy movement. When it is held back, it shows in the face as tension: a furrowed brow, a set jaw, lines that come from expression. This session focuses on letting that tension go.

    Signature serum: Apple stem cell extract, for renewal and new growth

  2. Session 2

    Fire

    Radiance

    Redness and a lack of glow

    Fire relates to the Heart, which Chinese medicine says shows in the complexion. Too much heat shows as redness and irritation; too little, as dullness. This session calms redness and brings back a natural glow.

    Signature serum: A cooling, heat-clearing botanical serum

  3. Session 3

    Earth

    Lift

    Puffiness and sagging

    Earth holds things in place and keeps fluids moving. When it is weak, the face can look puffy or start to lose its shape. This session focuses on firmness and lift.

    Signature serum: Shiitake mushroom extract

  4. Session 4

    Metal

    Refine

    Texture and pores

    In Chinese medicine, Metal governs the skin and the pores. This session focuses on smoothness, texture, and pore size.

    Signature serum: White snow fungus (tremella) extract, prized for moisture

  5. Session 5

    Water

    Restore

    Dark circles and deeper signs of ageing

    Water is the body's deepest reserve, and Chinese medicine links it to ageing. It shows around the eyes as dark circles and hollowness. The final session works on these deeper signs and seals in what the series has built.

    Signature serum: A deeply nourishing botanical serum
