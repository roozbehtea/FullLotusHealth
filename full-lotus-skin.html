Common questions

Is there downtime?

Nanoneedling has no downtime, so you can go straight back to your day. Microneedling can leave mild redness, similar to a light sunburn, for 1 to 2 days.

Who performs the treatment?

Roozbeh Ghobadpour, L.Ac., a licensed acupuncturist with over 10 years of clinical experience. You see him for the whole visit.

What happens at a first visit?

Your first visit includes an intake and a treatment plan. Please complete the new patient form before you come, so the visit can begin with what matters. The first visit counts toward a 3- or 5-session package.

What should I keep off my skin the morning of?

Come with a clean face. That morning, skip makeup, self-tanner, retinoids, and exfoliating acids. If you use a prescription product on your skin, note it on the intake form.
