Healing beyond the surface

Acupuncture is more than a physical treatment; it is a method of regulating the nervous system. By gently stimulating specific points, we signal the body to shift from a state of stress into deep restoration. This physiological reset supports healthy circulation and a return to your natural state of whole-body harmony and lasting vitality.

Your first visit

What to expect

  1. Consultation

    We begin with a deep dive into your health history, lifestyle, and goals to create a personalized treatment mapping.

  2. Gentle placement

    Ultra-fine, sterile needles are gently placed. Most patients feel only a slight sensation or nothing at all.

  3. Restorative rest

    You will rest for 20 to 30 minutes in a calm environment, allowing the needles to stimulate your body's healing response.

  4. Post-care ritual

    We conclude with herbal recommendations and a moment in our tea ceremony space for deep integration.
