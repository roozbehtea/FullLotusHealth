The five elements

A session for each element

Chinese medicine describes the face through five elements. Each session in the series follows one.

  • Wood: Tension and fine lines

    A session for the tension and lines that gather around the brow and eyes. Its own signature serum.

  • Fire: Redness and glow

    A session to calm redness and bring back warmth and radiance. Its own signature serum.

  • Earth: Puffiness and sagging

    A session for puffiness and for skin that has lost its lift. Its own signature serum.

  • Metal: Texture and pores

    A session to refine texture and the look of pores. Its own signature serum.

  • Water: Dark circles and ageing

    A session for dark circles and for the deeper signs of ageing. Its own signature serum.

How it works

Five sessions, one plan

  1. Intake and plan

    Your first visit starts with an intake, so the series is shaped around your skin.

  2. Five sessions, five focuses

    Each session works on one element's concern with its own serum, and the five together cover the whole face.

  3. Results build

    Sessions are spaced several weeks apart, so your skin has time to renew between visits.
