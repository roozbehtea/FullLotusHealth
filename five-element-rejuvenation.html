Session 1 of 5 · Malachite green

🌿 Wood: Clarify, even, move

Wood is the element of growth and movement. In the skin, it is the session for clearing and evening out.

Leads with: Congestion, uneven tone, puffiness

What you may notice: A fresh, clear look. Skin feels lighter and tone looks more even.

Session 2 of 5 · Cinnabar red

🔥 Fire: Calm and cool

Fire is warmth and radiance. In the skin, it is the session for settling heat and reactivity.

Leads with: Redness, heat, reactive skin

What you may notice: A calmer, cooler look, with a quiet glow.

Session 3 of 5 · Aged gold

🌾 Earth: Lift and nourish

Earth is the center. It grounds the whole journey, so every session begins with Earth's nourishment and is then tuned by one other element.

Session 4 of 5 · Pearl white

Leads with: Dehydration, fine lines

Common questions

What does Five Element mean here?

Chinese medicine describes nature, and the skin, through five elements: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Here they are a simple language for what your skin is doing right now. They are not a diagnosis.

What is a session like?

Every journey starts with an intake. Each session then leads with one element, with Earth's nourishment as the grounding base. Red light therapy is part of the treatment.

Why is this less invasive than ordinary cosmetic microneedling?

Sessions are paced several weeks apart and matched to what your skin needs that day, rather than run as a one-size-fits-all package. Nanoneedling stays at the surface and has no downtime.

What is the serum?

Each element has its own signature serum, chosen for what that element's session is meant to do for the skin. It is part of the session, and your plan is set at your first visit.

Why is it beneficial?

Skin does best when it is given time to renew between visits, and when each session has one clear job. The journey gives the series a shape, so you are not repeating the same treatment five times.

What are the short-term results?

Most people notice skin that looks fresher and feels more hydrated in the days after a session. Microneedling can leave mild pinkness for a day or so. Nanoneedling has no downtime.

What are the long-term results?

Results build as the sessions add up. Skin tends to look firmer, smoother and more even over the course of the series. Individual results vary.

Why did you create it?

I wanted a way to bring the thinking of Chinese medicine into skin care that feels calm, personal and unhurried, and to give each visit its own purpose.

Do I have to do all five?

The journey is designed as five sessions, one for each element. Book all five and the fifth session is free, which is 20% off.
