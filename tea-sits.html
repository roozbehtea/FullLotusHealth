Before you come

What happens at a tea sit?

We sit on cushions around the tea table and drink several bowls or cups of tea together, mostly in silence. No experience is needed. It's a simple way to slow down and meditate with tea.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can sit on the floor in. A cushion or low stool is provided.

Where is it?

In the tea space at Full Lotus Health, 2116 N Glenoaks Blvd, Ste 3, Burbank, CA 91504. Please arrive a few minutes early.

Can I bring friends?

Yes. You can reserve up to three seats at once. For a group of your own, book a private ceremony.

What if I can't make it?

Reply to your confirmation email or text (818) 253-9307 so your seat can go to someone else.

Prefer a private ceremony?

Gong fu and bowl tea ceremonies for you and up to five guests.
