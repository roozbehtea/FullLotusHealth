Ten years ago this fall, I opened the doors of Full Lotus Health on Glenoaks Blvd in Burbank. It was October 2016. I had a treatment room, a table, my needles and herbs, and a lot of hope. A decade later I'm still here, still the only practitioner, and still grateful every time someone walks up those steps.

This post is a thank-you, and a short look back at how the practice has grown.

## Where it started

Full Lotus Health began as a small acupuncture and Chinese herbal medicine practice. I had finished my master's in Traditional Chinese Medicine at Yo San University in Los Angeles the year before, and I wanted a place where people could slow down and be properly listened to.

That's still the heart of the work. Whether someone comes in for pain, stress, sleep or digestion, the visit starts with a conversation. In Chinese medicine, the way you eat, sleep and carry stress is all part of the picture, and it takes time to see that picture clearly.

My own roots in Chinese medicine lean toward the Earth school of Li Dong Yuan, which places digestion and nourishment at the center. That way of thinking still shapes how I care for people, and it's one reason I ask so much about how you eat, rest and recover.

## How the practice has grown

Over the years, skin became a bigger part of what I do. It began with [cosmetic acupuncture](/cosmetic-acupuncture-burbank), where very fine needles along the face, neck and body work on how your skin looks today and on the things behind it, like stress and sleep.

Later I added [nanoneedling](/nanoneedling-burbank) and [microneedling](/microneedling-burbank). Both are done by me, in the same quiet room, with custom serums chosen for your skin and red light therapy as part of every session. More recently these grew into the [Five Element Rejuvenation series](/five-element-rejuvenation), five sessions that each have their own focus and their own serum.

Tea has grown alongside the clinic, too. For years I've served gongfu tea in the small tea space here, mostly for patients after a treatment. Since 2024 I've also been the head Chinese medicine writer for Global Tea Hut, a tea magazine. Now I'm opening the tea space to everyone with [tea sits](/tea-sits): a small group around one table, sharing several steepings of good tea, mostly in quiet. No experience needed.

## What hasn't changed

Some things have stayed the same on purpose.

- It's still a one-practitioner clinic, so you see me every time.
- Visits are still unhurried, with time to talk before anything else happens.
- The room is still quiet, and the kettle is usually on.

People sometimes tell me their visit is the calmest hour of their week. After ten years, that's still the nicest thing anyone could say about this place, and it's what I try to protect every day.

## Thank you

To everyone in Burbank, Glendale, Toluca Lake, North Hollywood and beyond who has trusted me with your care over these ten years: thank you. A small practice like this one lives on trust and on kind words passed from one person to the next, and you've given me plenty of both.

To my patients who've been coming since the early days, thank you for growing with the clinic. To the people who found me last month, welcome. I'm glad you're here.
