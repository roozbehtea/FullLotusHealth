Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses fine, sterile, medical-grade needles to create tiny controlled channels in the skin. This prompts your skin's natural repair process, which builds new collagen over the following weeks.

### What it can help with

- Fine lines and wrinkles, including crow's feet
- Acne scarring
- Uneven texture and tone
- Enlarged pores
- Thinning hair along the hairline (scalp microneedling)

### What makes it different here

Every session uses custom Chinese herbal serums chosen for your skin, which absorb through the open channels during treatment. Red light therapy is included. Because your practitioner is trained in Chinese medicine, your skin is looked at as part of your whole health, not in isolation.

### What to expect

Sessions are 90 minutes, and your first visit includes an intake and a treatment plan. A numbing cream is applied first, and most people find the treatment comfortable. Expect some redness afterward, similar to a mild sunburn, that usually settles within a day or two. Results build over time, and most people do a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart.

### Microneedling or nanoneedling?

Microneedling works deeper, for scarring, lines, and thinning hair. Nanoneedling is a gentler surface treatment for glow and hydration, with no downtime. Not sure which fits you? Book a session and we'll decide together, or text or email a question.
