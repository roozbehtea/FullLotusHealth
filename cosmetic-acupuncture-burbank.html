Whatever resides in the heart is expressed in the face

In Chinese medicine the mind is represented by the heart, and it opens to the face. In this cosmetic acupuncture treatment, we calm the mind, balance the body, and focus on specific tension-releasing and lifting points on the face. Red light therapy is included.

What the treatment is

The Full Lotus Lift is a holistic, personalized beauty treatment designed to bring out your natural, radiant glow. It uses premium organic products and gentle techniques to nourish the skin while promoting a profound sense of inner calm.

Who gives it

Common questions

What is cosmetic acupuncture?

Facial acupuncture at tension-releasing and lifting points, combined with constitutional body points and red light therapy.

Should I book one session or a series?

Cosmetic acupuncture is best done as a series. Your first session includes a detailed intake and treatment plan.

How is it different from microneedling?

Cosmetic acupuncture works with acupuncture points and the mind-body connection. Microneedling and nanoneedling work on the skin's surface and layers with herbal serums.
