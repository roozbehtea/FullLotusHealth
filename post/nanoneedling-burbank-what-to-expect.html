What to expect from nanoneedling in Burbank

By Roozbeh Ghobadpour, L.Ac. · November 18, 2026 · 3 min read

Of all the skin work I do at Full Lotus Health, nanoneedling is the treatment I’m most excited about right now. It’s gentle, there’s no downtime, and people tend to leave looking rested and fresh. If you’ve been curious about it, here’s what it is, who it suits, and what a session with me looks like.

What nanoneedling is

Nanoneedling uses a tool with ultra-fine, nano-scale tips. Instead of reaching into the deeper layers of the skin the way microneedling does, it works only at the very surface. The tips open microscopic channels there, and those channels let a custom serum absorb where your skin needs it.

Because it stays at the surface, there’s nothing to recover from. Most people go straight back to work, out to dinner, or home to rest. Skin often looks brighter right away and keeps looking better over the next few days.

Who it suits

Nanoneedling is a good fit if:

  • your skin looks dull, dry or tired
  • you want a fresh, even look before an event
  • you’re new to needling treatments and want a gentle first step
  • your skin is on the sensitive side, or hasn’t done well with stronger treatments
  • you’re between microneedling sessions and want to keep your skin looking its best

If you’re hoping to work on deeper concerns like scarring or texture, microneedling may suit you better. I wrote a longer guide to microneedling vs nanoneedling if you’d like to compare the two.

What a session looks like

A nanoneedling session at Full Lotus Health is 60 minutes, in a quiet private room on Glenoaks Blvd. Here’s how the hour usually goes.

We talk first. I start by looking at your skin and asking how you’ve been: sleep, stress, digestion, the season. I’m a licensed acupuncturist trained in Chinese medicine, and in my experience what’s happening inside usually shows up on the face.

I choose your serum. Based on what I see, I choose a custom serum for your skin that day. Not every face needs the same thing, and your skin may want something different next month.

The nanoneedling itself. I work the nano-tips gently across your skin while pressing the serum in. Most people find it comfortable, and many find it relaxing.

Red light therapy. Every session includes red light therapy, which helps build collagen. Clients love resting under it while the serums penetrate deeply into the skin.

Back to your day. There’s no downtime, so you can head straight back out. Visits here are unhurried, and they often end with a cup of tea.

One session or a series?

Nanoneedling works well as a single visit, for example before a wedding, a trip or a big day at work. It also works as a regular series, where your skin gets steady attention and results build gently from one visit to the next.

If you’d like a series with more shape to it, my Five Element Rejuvenation series is five sessions, each with its own focus and its own serum, and you can build it around nanoneedling. Five Element Rejuvenation™ series: 5 nanoneedling sessions for $1,400 ($350 less than five single sessions).

Why see an acupuncturist for your skin?

Plenty of places offer skin treatments. What I bring is over ten years as a licensed acupuncturist, and a way of looking at your skin as part of your whole health rather than in isolation. You’ll be treated by me every time, in the same calm room, and we get to know your skin together over time.
