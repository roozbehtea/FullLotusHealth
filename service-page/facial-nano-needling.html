Nanoneedling

Unveil your skin's natural glow with advanced nano-infusion technology.

Nanoneedling facial in Burbank, performed by Roozbeh Ghobadpour, L.Ac., a licensed acupuncturist with over 10 years of clinical experience.

Nanoneedling is a gentle alternative to microneedling. It uses ultra-fine nano-tips that open microscopic channels in the very surface of the skin, without going deeper. Those channels let hyaluronic acid, vitamins, and custom Chinese herbal serums absorb where your skin needs them.

What it can help with

  • Dull, dehydrated, or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and uneven texture
  • Sensitive skin that doesn't do well with stronger treatments
  • A glow before an event
  • Maintenance between microneedling sessions

What makes it different here

Every treatment uses custom Chinese herbal serums chosen for your skin, and red light therapy is included. Because your practitioner is trained in Chinese medicine, your skin is looked at as part of your whole health, not in isolation.

What to expect

Sessions are 60 minutes. There's no downtime, so you can go straight back to your day. Skin often looks brighter right away and keeps improving over the following days. It works well on its own or as a regular series.

Nanoneedling or microneedling?

Nanoneedling is a surface-level treatment for glow and hydration, with no downtime. Microneedling works deeper, for scarring, lines, and thinning hair. Not sure which fits you? Book a session and we'll decide together, or text or email a question.
