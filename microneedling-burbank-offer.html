Common questions

Does it hurt?

A gentle topical numbing goes on first. Most people find the visit comfortable. You may feel a light scratching sensation.

What is the downtime?

Skin can look flushed, similar to a mild sunburn. Botanical topicals and red light help calm that post-treatment redness. The flush usually settles within a day or two.

What if I want zero needles or zero downtime?

Needle-free nanoneedling is available on request at your visit. It stays at the surface, for glow and hydration, and has no downtime. Ask when you come in.
