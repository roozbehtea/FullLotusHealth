Support, alongside your doctor

Fertility care at Full Lotus Health is support, not a replacement for your doctor or fertility clinic. Many people use acupuncture and Chinese medicine while trying naturally, or alongside IVF or IUI, to support their cycle, sleep, and stress through what can be a demanding time.

What we can help with

  • Preparing your body in the months before trying, or before an IVF cycle
  • Irregular or painful cycles
  • Stress, sleep, and the emotional weight of trying to conceive
  • Support around IVF and IUI, timed to your cycle
  • Partners too: fertility is a two-person picture

How it works

  1. A detailed intake

    We go through your health history, your cycle, and any testing and treatment so far. Bring recent test results to your first visit if you have them.

  2. A plan for your timeline

    Usually regular acupuncture, often weekly, with herbal medicine where it fits. If you're doing IVF, sessions are planned around your clinic's dates.

  3. Steady support

    Visits are unhurried and often end with tea. It is a calm place to come back to while you go through the process.

What we won't promise

No one can honestly promise a pregnancy, and we won't. What we offer is careful, consistent support that works with your medical care, not against it.

Individual results vary.

Common questions

Can I have acupuncture during IVF?

Yes, many people do. Sessions are planned around your clinic's schedule. Please let your fertility clinic know you're having acupuncture.

Can I take Chinese herbs during IVF?

Only with your fertility clinic's agreement. Many clinics ask patients to pause herbs during a cycle, and we follow their guidance.

How often will I come in?

Often weekly, with extra sessions timed to your cycle or IVF dates. Your plan is set at your first visit.
